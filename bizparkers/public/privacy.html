Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or request a free audit, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone number, email (if you add it), the services you're interested in, your budget range and anything you tell us about your business.

We also count visits to this website to understand which pages are useful. This uses a random ID stored in your browser, not cookies, and does not identify you personally. We do not use Google Analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To prepare a proposal, quote or audit you asked for.
  • To improve our website and services.

We never sell or rent your details, and we don't add you to mailing lists you didn't ask for.

Where it's stored

Your details are stored securely on our own server and are only seen by the Bizparkers team.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Just message us using the contact details on our website.
