FAQ

Good questions

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message and a real person will reply within 24 hours.

How much does a website or app cost?+

It depends on what you need. A simple business website costs far less than a custom app with logins and payments. After a free call we send a fixed, written quote, and that is the price you pay.

How long does a project take?+

A landing page can go live in about a week. Business websites usually take 2 to 4 weeks, online stores 3 to 6 weeks, and mobile apps or web apps 6 to 12 weeks depending on features. You get the timeline with your quote.

Do I own the website, app and logo files?+

Yes. Once the project is paid, the code, design files, domain and accounts are yours. We set everything up in your name.

Can AI really help a small business?+

Yes. The most common wins are answering customer questions on WhatsApp, capturing and following up on leads automatically, and cutting manual data entry. We start with the task that costs you the most time.

How soon will SEO show results?+

Local SEO and Google Business Profile changes often show movement within a few weeks. Competitive keywords usually take 3 to 6 months. We report rankings and traffic every month so you can see progress.

Do you offer support after launch?+

Yes. We offer monthly plans for updates, hosting, backups, SEO and marketing, or you can call us only when you need something.
